---
name: apersona
description: Use Apersona to connect a persona, recall experiences, apply preferences to decisions, record owner corrections, upload material and confirm or decline reading, or turn the integration on or off.
---

Use the MCP connection bundled with this Antigravity plugin. Find its namespaced `opening`, `lookup` and `recall` tools; other tools may require lazy discovery. If authentication is required, ask the owner to authenticate this server under Customizations → Installed → MCP Servers. Do not request an API key or create a second connection.

Call `opening` once for this chat and follow any returned cursor until complete. Keep its returned `conversation_id` for later calls; never invent an identifier. Installation and OAuth alone do not prove access: confirm a successful opening reply. An empty persona is not an authentication error.

Continue the owner's actual task. Use `lookup` for judgment and `recall` for sourced original words, following the live schemas. Separate source evidence from model inference (`situation_source=client_model`). If asked to change workspaces, use `workspace_status` and `select_workspace`: the owner's request is the consent, one call switches the whole workspace, and one conversation never uses two. A usable default persona needs no workspace setup.

When the task follows the owner's working documents or needs how similar past cases went, call `skill_packs` to see the packs, then `ask_skill`: a passage pack takes `question`; a case pack takes only the known conditions in `case` (`case_source=owner` only for conditions the owner just stated). Not found is said as such; do not fill it in. Report with `report_skill_outcome` and the reply's query identifier only after the owner says what they did; never overrule or deny for them. `skill_scorecard` shows each pack's record. Reading a document into cases spends credit: call `read_skill_source` without `consent_sha256`, show the returned sentence and maximum cost, and call again with that hash only after the owner's explicit agreement. Use `define_skill_pack` only when the owner asks for a pack of their own.

On later recall calls, return only receipt identifiers from complete replies actually received through this same connection in `received`, retaining the conversation identifier. Missing, truncated or timed-out replies are not acknowledged. Receipt is not evidence of adoption.

Answer with sources, reasons and uncertainties. Missing evidence remains unknown. These packages do not automatically capture messages or AI answers. Capture requires explicit owner consent; paid reading requires the owner's agreement to its estimate. A `connector_read_only` rejection means the owner must enable uploads and modifications for this connection in the site's settings; do not create another login to bypass it.

When the owner corrects a judgment ("that is not me" or "I would not do that"), discover `claim` and call it with `kind="correction"`, their own words in `text`, and the applicable `stake` from the live schema. Include only context they supplied; do not invent a registration identifier or correction reason. To withdraw a correction at their request, use its returned number in `withdraws` and their withdrawal words in `text`. Check the tool reply before saying the correction was saved. Owner corrections remain available on read-only connections.

When asked to upload material, discover `upload_files` and send only the requested files, each with `name` and `text` or `base64`, following its live schema. Uploading prepares material; it does not authorize reading. Show the returned file results, estimate or free allowance, and exact consent question. If needed, retrieve them with `upload_result(job_id=...)`. Wait for the owner's answer, including when reading is free. If they agree, call `agree_upload` with the returned `job_id`, `consent_sha256`, and their exact words as `owner_reply`; if they refuse, call `decline_upload` with `job_id` and their exact `owner_reply`. Silence is neither consent nor refusal. Use `job` to check agreed reading progress. Declining reading does not delete the uploaded originals.

To turn this integration off or on, use Antigravity native plugin management under Customizations → Installed; use the same host management to remove it. These controls do not enable automatic capture or revoke OAuth. Revoke authorization separately in the site's authorized connections settings. Do not use Claude `/apersona:on` or `/apersona:off` commands here.

For an owner-authorized continuing document or dialogue index, call upload_files with a fixed source_id, source_author, workspace_id and filename. Set workdoc_only=true for dialogue indexing. Show the first returned consent sentence and obtain explicit agreement once. Keep that upload job_id as source_grant for later versions within the same scope. Preserve distinct repeated messages and their locations. A source_denied response ends continuation under that grant. Taking back the source through the site withdraws its versions. Paid case reading follows its separate cost authorization.
